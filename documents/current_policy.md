# TECHPULSE SKILL ACADEMY
**Document type: Student policy**
- Policy version: 2.0
- Effective date: 01 November 2026
- Status: CURRENT
- Owner: Administration Department

This document is the controlling policy for questions about rules currently in force. It replaces version 1.0 from 01 June 2026.


## 1. Registration and fee payment
A learner submits the registration form and pays the published course fee before the first class. Administration confirms the seat after checking the form and payment. The standard registration deadline is 3 calendar days before the published course start date. Late registration may be considered only if a seat remains and the academic coordinator approves it. The assistant cannot approve late registration.


## 2. Attendance
Learners should attend at least 80% of scheduled sessions to qualify for the standard course-completion certificate. If a learner misses a class because of an emergency, the learner should contact administration within 2 working days. An absence is not automatically excused; administration records the reason and decides whether supporting evidence is needed.


## 3. Two attendance exceptions
Exception A: If the academy cancels a class, that cancelled session is removed from the learner's attendance denominator and a replacement session is announced.
Exception B: If a learner has an approved medical or family emergency, administration may arrange one catch-up activity. This does not automatically waive the 80% attendance requirement. The learner must contact administration; the assistant cannot approve the exception.


## 4. Refunds and withdrawals
A learner who withdraws at least 7 calendar days before the course start date may request a refund of 80% of the course fee. A withdrawal request made fewer than 7 calendar days before the start date is not eligible for a standard refund. If the academy cancels a course and cannot offer a suitable replacement, the learner may request a full refund. All refunds require administration review and are normally processed within 10 working days after approval. The assistant cannot approve or promise a refund.


## 5. Class timing and office hours
The front desk is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Regular class times are shown in each course outline. Public office hours are not a guarantee that a particular instructor is available without an appointment.


## 6. Certificates
A learner must meet the 80% attendance requirement, submit the required final project, and clear any outstanding course fee before the standard completion certificate is issued. The academy normally issues an approved certificate within 5 working days after the final assessment result is recorded. The assistant cannot check an individual learner's certificate status.


## 7. Learning materials and conduct
Digital notes are for enrolled learners' personal study. Learners must not share another learner's personal information or submit copied work as their own. Respectful conduct is required in class and in academy communication channels. Serious conduct concerns are referred to the academic coordinator.


## 8. Exact operational facts
The standard registration deadline is 3 calendar days before the course start. The standard attendance threshold is 80%. A qualifying early withdrawal refund request is 80% of the course fee when submitted at least 7 calendar days before the start date. Approved refunds are normally processed within 10 working days.


## 9. Policy authority
For current-rule questions, use this version 2.0 policy. If an older policy says something different, explain that version 1.0 is archived and version 2.0 is current. If a question asks for an exception or personal decision, refer the learner to administration instead of claiming approval.
