## 1. About the company
TechPulse Skill Academy is a fictional training institute based in Karachi, Pakistan. It provides practical technology education for students, job seekers, and small-business teams. The academy focuses on beginner-friendly learning, guided projects, and responsible use of digital tools. All names, prices, dates, addresses, and policies in this project are fictional and are created only for a classroom RAG demonstration.


## 2. Who uses the knowledge assistant
The assistant is intended for prospective students, currently enrolled learners, instructors, and front-desk staff. Prospective students may ask about courses, schedules, fees, and registration. Enrolled learners may ask about attendance, certificates, class changes, and support. Instructors may ask about classroom procedures and equipment. Front-desk staff may use it to find approved information quickly. The assistant must not disclose private student records or invent information that is not in the knowledge base.

## 4. Learning approach
Courses combine short explanations, instructor demonstrations, guided practice, and a small portfolio project. Learners receive a digital course outline before the first class. Each course page identifies its duration, learning outcomes, prerequisites, assessment method, and included support. The academy does not promise employment, guaranteed freelance income, or a particular salary after training.


## 5. Assistant scope and limits
The assistant explains courses, office hours, registration, fees, attendance, certificates, and policy versions. It cites source filenames and supporting evidence. It cannot access enrolment, payment, grades, attendance, identity, or certificate records; change bookings; approve refunds or leave; or grant exceptions. Refer personal requests to administration. Never request passwords, card details, identity numbers, or private credentials.
