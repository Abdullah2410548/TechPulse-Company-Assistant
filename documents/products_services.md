# TECHPULSE SKILL ACADEMY
**Document type: Products and services catalogue**
- Document version: 1.0
- Effective date: 01 November 2026
- Status: Current
- Owner: Academic Department

All offerings, durations, and fees below are fictional classroom data.


## 1. Web Development Foundations
Duration: 8 weeks. Fee: PKR 18,000. Learners practise HTML, CSS, responsive page layouts, and basic JavaScript. The final activity is a small multi-page portfolio website. Prerequisite: basic computer use.


## 2. Python Programming Basics
Duration: 6 weeks. Fee: PKR 15,000. Topics include variables, conditions, loops, functions, lists, dictionaries, and reading simple text files. Learners complete a command-line mini project. Prerequisite: basic computer use.


## 3. Generative AI Fundamentals
Duration: 4 weeks. Fee: PKR 12,000. Learners explore language models, prompting, limitations, responsible use, and simple API-based demonstrations. The course does not promise access to paid APIs; free-tier availability may change.


## 4. Agentic AI and Workflow Design
Duration: 6 weeks. Fee: PKR 17,500. Learners study tool calling, task decomposition, agent safety, and workflow evaluation. The final project is a small assistant that uses a clearly defined data source. Prerequisite: basic Python familiarity.


## 5. Graphic Design Essentials
Duration: 6 weeks. Fee: PKR 14,000. Learners practise layout, typography, colour basics, social media graphics, and export formats. The portfolio task is a set of three promotional designs.


## 6. Data Analysis with Spreadsheets
Duration: 5 weeks. Fee: PKR 13,000. Learners clean small datasets, use formulas, sort and filter records, and create charts. The course uses sample data and does not require access to a real company's private records.


## 7. Digital Marketing Basics
Duration: 4 weeks. Fee: PKR 11,000. Topics include audience research, content calendars, basic search concepts, campaign measurement, and ethical advertising. The final activity is a sample campaign plan.


## 8. UI Design with Figma
Duration: 5 weeks. Fee: PKR 13,500. Learners create wireframes, reusable components, and a clickable prototype. The course focuses on interface structure and usability rather than professional certification.


## 9. Short workshops
The academy may announce one-day workshops on topics such as Git and GitHub, CV preparation, or AI productivity. Workshop dates and fees are announced separately; no fixed price is promised in this catalogue.


## 10. Enrolment and support
A learner can request the course outline by email or visit the front desk during office hours. A seat is confirmed only after administration verifies the registration form and payment status. The assistant can explain the public process but cannot verify a person's individual payment or reserve a seat.


## 11. What is included
Unless a course outline says otherwise, the fee includes scheduled teaching sessions, digital learning notes, and one final project review. Personal laptops, internet packages, paid software subscriptions, transport, and external examination fees are not included.


## 12. Course changes
Course dates may change if an instructor is unavailable or the minimum class size is not reached. Administration informs registered learners through the contact details they supplied. The assistant must not invent a replacement date; it should direct learners to administration for a confirmed schedule.
