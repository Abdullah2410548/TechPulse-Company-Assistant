# TECHPULSE SKILL ACADEMY
**Document type: Archived student policy**
- Policy version: 1.0
- Effective date: 01 June 2026
- Status: ARCHIVED — NOT CURRENT
- Owner: Administration Department

This historical document is retained only to answer questions that explicitly ask about the previous policy. It was replaced by policy version 2.0 effective 01 November 2026. Do not apply these rules to present-day cases.


## 1. Registration
Under version 1.0, the standard registration deadline was 5 calendar days before the published course start date. Late registration was not described as an available exception and required a direct decision from administration.


## 2. Attendance
Under version 1.0, learners were expected to attend at least 75% of scheduled sessions to qualify for the standard course-completion certificate. The policy did not describe the two attendance exceptions now listed in version 2.0.


## 3. Refunds and withdrawals
Under version 1.0, a learner who withdrew at least 10 calendar days before the course start date could request a refund of 70% of the course fee. A request made fewer than 10 calendar days before the start date was not eligible for the standard refund. If the academy cancelled a course, the learner could request a refund after administration review.


## 4. Refund processing
Version 1.0 did not specify a normal number of working days for processing an approved refund. Staff were asked to provide an estimate after reviewing the case. Do not invent a historical processing time.


## 5. Certificates
Version 1.0 required at least 75% attendance, submission of the final project, and clearance of outstanding fees before the standard certificate could be issued. It did not state the current five-working-day target for issuing an approved certificate.


## 6. Historical comparison
The rules changed in version 2.0, effective 01 November 2026. The attendance threshold changed from 75% to 80%. The registration deadline changed from 5 calendar days to 3 calendar days before the course start. The early-withdrawal refund changed from 70% at least 10 calendar days before the start to 80% at least 7 calendar days before the start. The current policy also describes two attendance exceptions and a normal refund processing target of 10 working days.


## 7. How to answer historical questions
If a user asks "what was the old rule?" or specifically mentions version 1.0, quote this archived policy and label it historical. If a user asks "what is the rule now?", use current_policy.md. If the question does not make clear whether the user wants the old or current rule, prefer the current policy and mention that an archived version differs where relevant.


## 8. Limits
This document is not proof of any individual learner's eligibility for a refund, certificate, attendance adjustment, or registration. Personal cases must be reviewed by administration.
