## 1. How can I ask about a course?
Read the course catalogue and request the course outline from hello@techpulse.example or visit the front desk during office hours. The outline contains the schedule, learning outcomes, prerequisites, and assessment method.


## 2. How do I register?
Choose a course, complete the registration form supplied by administration, and follow the published payment instructions. A seat is confirmed only after administration verifies the form and payment. The assistant cannot accept forms or confirm payments.


## 3. Can I join after the registration deadline?
The current policy allows administration to consider a late registration only if a seat remains and the academic coordinator approves it. Contact administration. The assistant cannot make the decision.


## 4. What if a class is cancelled?
Administration informs registered learners and announces a replacement session. Under current policy, an academy-cancelled session is removed from the attendance denominator. Learners should check the message sent by administration for the replacement details.


## 5. What if I am absent?
Contact administration within 2 working days if an emergency caused the absence. An approved medical or family emergency may lead to one catch-up activity, but it does not automatically waive the attendance requirement. The assistant cannot approve an absence.


## 6. How do I request a refund?
Send a withdrawal request to administration and include the course name and registration reference. Do not send passwords, card numbers, or other payment credentials. Administration checks the request against the current policy and confirms the decision. The assistant can explain the published rule but cannot approve a refund.


## 7. How do I receive a certificate?
Meet the current attendance threshold, submit the final project, and clear outstanding course fees. Administration records the final result and issues an approved certificate according to the current policy timeline. The assistant cannot check personal certificate records.


## 8. What if the assistant cannot answer?
It should say it could not find enough information in the provided documents and refer the user to administration. It must not invent facts.


## 9. Can the assistant change my booking or payment record?
No. It cannot access student accounts, change bookings, accept payments, or edit personal records.


## 10. What information should I never share in chat?
Never share passwords, card details, identity document numbers, private student records, or account recovery codes.


## 11. What if two documents disagree?
Use current_policy.txt for current rules and previous_policy.txt only for explicit historical questions. Explain unresolved conflicts and refer them to administration.


## 12. Can I get a guaranteed job after a course?
No job or income guarantee is stated in the documents.


## 13. What if I need an instructor?
Ask administration to arrange contact or an appointment; public office hours do not guarantee an instructor is available.
